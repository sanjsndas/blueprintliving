Privacy Policy

Blueprint Living Privacy Policy

Blueprint Living respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, store and share personal information when you contact us or use our interior design services in England.

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide, such as your name, email address, telephone number, postal address, project requirements, property details, budget, and any messages or files you send us. We may also collect basic technical information when you visit our website, such as your IP address, browser type and information about how you use the site.

How we use your information

We use personal information to respond to enquiries, discuss and deliver interior design services, prepare proposals and manage projects, communicate with you, process payments where applicable, maintain business records, and improve our services and website. We may also use information where necessary to comply with legal obligations or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Legal grounds for processing

Under UK data protection law, we rely on one or more of the following legal grounds: taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, performing a contract with you, complying with a legal obligation, pursuing our legitimate business interests, or your consent where consent is required. You may withdraw consent at any time.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with service providers who help us operate our business, such as website hosting, email, payment, accounting or project-management providers. We may also disclose information where required by law or where necessary to protect our rights. Service providers are expected to handle information securely and only for permitted purposes.

International transfers

If a service provider processes your information outside the United Kingdom, we will take appropriate steps to ensure that your information receives protection in accordance with applicable data protection law.

How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including to provide services, maintain business and financial records, resolve disputes and meet legal requirements. The appropriate retention period depends on the type of information and the circumstances.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, ask us to correct or delete it, restrict or object to its processing, or request a copy in a portable format. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it. To exercise your rights, contact Blueprint Living using the contact details through which you usually communicate with us. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Cookies and website technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to make the site work, understand its use and, where applicable, support optional features. You can manage cookies through your browser settings and any cookie controls provided on the website. Some features may not work properly if certain cookies are disabled.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's information

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children unless it is provided by a parent or guardian in connection with a project.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will apply from the date it is made available.

Contact

For questions about this policy or how we handle your personal information, please contact Blueprint Living using the contact details provided on our website or in your correspondence with us.
